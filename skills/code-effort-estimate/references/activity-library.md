# Activity library

Typical lines for a Code discovery / research / CX strategy estimate. Days are starting points from past estimates; scale by the driver and say how in the note.

## Immersion

Getting set up and reading in. Stakeholder interviews and team walkthroughs are **not** here; they go in Discovery.

| Activity | Typical days | Driver | Note pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 – prep | 1 | — | Preparing the kick-off: agenda, pre-read, attendee list |
| Day 0 | 5 | team size attending | Kick-off bringing stakeholders together to align on key facts, ambitions and measures of success |
| Doc review | 3 | volume of client docs | Review of existing research, strategy and performance documents |
| Competitor and market review | 6 | competitors × depth | Broad immersion across the market and competitors, establishing the facts for later work |

## Discovery / insight gathering

Stakeholder interviews, team "show me" walkthroughs of the systems, and all customer, user and field research.

| Activity | Typical days | Driver | Note pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team "show me" walkthroughs | 1 setup + 0.5 per session + 0.25 write-up | sessions | The team's day in the systems. Use `workshops` |
| Customer interviews / groups | 4 per group | groups | Planning, running and notes. Analysis sits in Synthesis |
| Customer survey / poll | 3–5 | questions, analysis depth | Design, build, launch, clean. Check if it can piggyback an existing survey |
| Internal mapping workshops | ~0.67 per 2-hr session | departments or teams | 2-hr session per team logging tech, systems, data, comms, time, blockers |
| Website / app user testing | 5 per group | test groups | Usability testing of the current site to understand strengths and weaknesses |
| Stakeholder interviews | 1 setup + 0.5 per interview + 0.2 analysis | interviewees (default 5) | Paired, 5 in a day. Use `stakeholder_interviews` |
| CX feedback data review | 1–2 | data available | Review of existing customer feedback (depends on access) |
| Analytics / BI review | 3–5 | data available | Volumes and values across the core journey (depends on access) |

**Expenses, not days:** participant recruitment (e.g. agency fee per group), incentives, travel, venues, tool licences. Enter as cost per unit × quantity.

## External costs (always ask, passed through at cost)

Recruitment partner (per group or participant) · incentives · equipment (test devices, recording or research kit) · prototyping tool licence · research or testing platform · survey tool · venue hire · travel. AI tokens are a percentage line in management, not an external cost.

## Management (calculated, never typed in; charged once on the whole project)

| Activity | Default | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery management | 20% | effort days, nearest half day |
| Project management (alternative) | 10% | cost of effort and fixed items, per phase |
| Product consultancy | 5% | effort days, nearest half day |
| AI token costs | 2% | effort cost, whole project |
| Contingency | 0% | sub total, allowance for unknowns |
